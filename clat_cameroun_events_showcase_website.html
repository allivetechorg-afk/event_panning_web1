Our Story & Cultural Heritage

Where Modern Luxury Meets Cameroonian Soul

Founded between Douala and Yaoundé, Éclat Cameroun Events was born from a singular vision: to elevate Cameroon’s vibrant celebratory culture onto the world stage of luxury event design.

Whether orchestrating a multi-day traditional Dot (Mariage Coutumier) steeped in ancestral protocol, curating elevated gastronomy featuring gourmet Ndolè, grilled Kribi lobster, and French haute cuisine, or producing international economic summits, every detail is choreographed with white-glove precision.
